GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do sessions cost?

Please get in touch via email and I'll be happy to discuss pricing. Rates are competitive and reflect 20 years of qualified teaching experience. I offer a free initial chat before any commitment.

How long is each session?

Sessions are typically one hour, though this can be adjusted to suit your child's needs and concentration levels — especially for younger students.

How often should my child have sessions?

Most students benefit from one session per week, but this can be increased during exam season or when catching up. We'll figure out what works best after our initial chat.

Do you offer online sessions?

Yes — online sessions are available via video call and are just as effective as in-person. I use a shared whiteboard so it feels natural and interactive.

How quickly will my child see results?

Most students notice an improvement in confidence within the first few sessions. Grade improvements typically show within a few weeks to a couple of months, depending on the starting point and frequency of sessions.

Is there a minimum commitment?

No — there's no long-term contract. I believe in earning your trust through results, not locking you in. You're free to stop at any time, though most students stay for many months.
